MealKind Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

MealKind respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the MealKind application.

Information We Collect

MealKind may collect account information such as your email address when you sign in. Information you provide when using MealKind may also be processed to provide the app's features.

Meal and Image Data

Meal descriptions and images you choose to analyze may be processed by third-party AI services to provide meal-related suggestions. Meal images are not sold to advertisers.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple and RevenueCat. MealKind does not directly store your payment card information.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, MealKind may use OneSignal to send reminders and app-related notifications.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only as necessary to operate the application.

Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated account data from within the app or by contacting us.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: aplusapps.app@gmail.com
